# Roadmap

## Compatible v2 releases

- Subtitle preservation and SRT/WebVTT embedding or burn-in
- Text/watermark layers and explicit font management
- Social presets, GIF/WebP previews, contact sheets, and waveforms
- Metadata and chapter editing
- Explicit NVENC, VideoToolbox, VAAPI, and Quick Sync capability detection
- Resource-bounded batch processing
- Streaming adapters for operations that do not require random access

## Future v3 exploration

A declarative multi-track timeline, keyframes, arbitrary filter graphs, plugins, and advanced color/audio workflows would materially expand the security and compatibility surface and therefore belong in a separately designed major release.
